Upcoming Events

  1. Ferry Village Walking Tour · Sat, Sep 26
  2. Waterfowl Youth Hunt Days at Beaver Island · Sat–Sun, Sep 27–28
  3. Citizen Preparedness Course – Golden Age Center · Mon, Sep 28
  4. One-on-One Tech Trainer Session – Grand Island Memorial Library · Tue, Oct 6
  5. GI Historical Society Meeting & Program · Thu, Oct 1
  6. GI Chamber 59th Annual Citizen of the Year Awards · Thu, Oct 8

1. 🌿 Ferry Village Walking Tour Sat, September 26 · 10:00 AM · Corner of Ferry Rd. and East River Rd. (by the hot dog stand) Sponsored by the GI Historic Preservation Advisory Board, this guided walking tour explores the historic Ferry Village neighborhood. The tour this year includes a stop at the Niagara River Station Fishing Club, a look inside a classic old-school fishing club. There is a $10 charge; call Sharon at 716-773-2421 for reservations.

2. 👨‍👩‍👧 Waterfowl Youth Hunt Days at Beaver Island State Park Sat–Sun, September 27–28 · Beaver Island State Park New York State's designated youth waterfowl hunting days give young hunters a chance to get out on the water before the regular duck and goose seasons open. Blind draws are held in-person at the park — young participants need proof of the Waterfowl ID course to enter the draw.

3. 🏛️ Citizen Preparedness Course – Golden Age Center Mon, September 28 · Grand Island Golden Age Center This NYS-sponsored emergency preparedness training walks participants through how to get ready for severe weather and other emergencies. The course is free and open to the public at the Golden Age Center.

4. 🏛️ GI Historical Society Meeting & Program Thu, October 1 · 7:00 PM · River Lea, Beaver Island State Park The Grand Island Historical Society's monthly meeting features a presentation by Alan Nowicki, Director of Programming at the Toycroft Campus. It's a great way to dig into local history with your neighbors.

5. 📚 One-on-One Tech Trainer Session – Grand Island Memorial Library Tue, October 6 · Grand Island Memorial Library, 1715 Bedell Rd. The library is offering free 45-minute one-on-one sessions with a Technology Trainer to help residents with devices, software, and online tools. Registration is required — call 716-773-7124 to book a time slot.

6. 🙌 GI Chamber 59th Annual Citizen of the Year Awards Thu, October 8 · Grand Island Chamber of Commerce The Chamber's annual awards night honors community members who have given their time and energy to make Grand Island a better place. This is the 59th year the ceremony has been held, making it one of the Island's longest-running community traditions.
