1. 🌿 20th Annual Paddles Up Niagara Saturday, July 25 · 7:00 AM – 1:00 PM · Beaver Island State Park

This family-friendly paddling event lets beginners and experienced kayakers alike explore the natural habitats of the Niagara River, launching from Beaver Island State Park.

Guides and safety paddlers are on hand throughout the morning, and the celebration wraps up with exhibitors, vendors, and free prize drawings.

A $7 park entrance fee applies after 9 AM.

2. 🎵 Tuesday Summer Concert: Barroom Buzzards Tuesday, July 28 · 6:30 PM · Marion Klingel Town Common, 2255 Baseline Rd

The Barroom Buzzards bring New Orleans jazz and Dixieland music to the Town Common stage.

The concert series is a free, family-friendly evening of live local entertainment put on by the Grand Island Recreation Department.

The Zonta Club runs a snack stand and 50/50 raffle on site.

3. 👨‍👩‍👧 Walk With Eagles — Hawk Creek Wildlife Center Wednesday, July 29 · 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM · Grand Island Memorial Library

Hawk Creek Wildlife Center is bringing two live eagle ambassadors for an up-close look — these are wild animals with sharp talons, so no touching.

Photos are encouraged, and the program includes a presentation, crafts, and a Q&A.

This event is open to all ages.

4. 👨‍👩‍👧 Llama Llama in the Library Monday, August 3 · 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM · Grand Island Memorial Library

Two llama representatives from Llama Adventures visit the library, and patrons of all ages are invited to say hello, touch and interact with the animals, and learn a bit about these unique creatures.

A great drop-in for little ones and curious kids of any age.

5. 🙌 National Night Out Tuesday, August 4 · 5:30 PM – 8:00 PM · Grand Island Town Hall / Town Common, 2255 Baseline Rd

Grand Island is hosting National Night Out at Town Hall on Tuesday, August 4 from 5:30 to 8:00 PM, a community celebration built around neighbors coming together.

The Recreation Department also lists the event at Town Common, where there will be activities and the Tuesday Night Concert also takes place that evening.

6. 🎵 Tuesday Summer Concert: Rock Angel Tuesday, August 4 · 6:30 PM · Marion Klingel Town Common, 2255 Baseline Rd

Rock Angel is a high-energy performance band covering rock, pop, blues, southern rock, country, funk, and a bit of jazz — the kind of set that has something for everyone in the crowd.

Free to attend, part of the Recreation Department's weekly summer series.

7. 📚 Treat Your Shelf Teen Book Club — Final Session Thursday, August 6 · 1:00 PM – 3:00 PM · Grand Island Memorial Library

This Thursday book club is geared toward teens entering grades 6–9 and meets from 1–3 PM for crafting, activities, and book trivia around the reading of "City of Spies" by James Ponti.

Books are purchased for participating teens.

This is the final Thursday session of the series.
