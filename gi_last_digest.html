1. 🏛️ GISD Board of Education Meeting & Workshop Mon, November 2 · 7:30 PM · Grand Island High School Professional Development Room, 1100 Ransom Road The Grand Island Central School District Board holds its monthly public meeting and workshop.

Community members are welcome to attend and observe.

2. 🍽️ Chipotle Fundraiser Tue, November 10 · 4:00 PM · Chipotle Mexican Grill, 2178 Grand Island Blvd A community fundraiser night at the Grand Island Chipotle location.

A portion of sales during the event goes back to the sponsoring organization.

3. 📚 Evening Book Club Tue, November 10 · 6:00 PM · Grand Island Memorial Library, 1715 Bedell Rd The library's evening book club meets monthly on the second Tuesday. All are welcome, and physical copies of the book are available to borrow.

The November selection is The Death of Mrs. Westaway by Ruth Ware.

4. 🏛️ Veterans Day Ceremony Wed, November 11 · 11:00 AM · Veterans Park, Grand Island The town's annual Veterans Day services honor those who served.

The event is free and open to the public.

5. 🌿 Mapping Invasives on Grand Island Thu, November 12 · 5:30 PM · Eco Island Nature Center, Grand Island A community gathering focused on identifying and mapping invasive species on the island.

This is a free event for anyone interested in local conservation efforts.

6. 📚 Tomb of the Unknown Soldier Thu, November 12 · 6:00 PM · Grand Island Memorial Library, 1715 Bedell Rd Presenter Theresa Wiater leads this library program. Registration is required — call 716-773-7124 to reserve a spot.

7. 🎨 Painting with Martha Thu, November 12 · Golden Age Center, Grand Island A guided painting session hosted at the Golden Age Center as part of the town's fall special events programming.

Check grandislandny.gov for time and registration details.

8. 👨‍👩‍👧 LEGO Club Sat, November 14 · 1:00 PM – 2:00 PM · Grand Island Memorial Library, 1715 Bedell Rd Kids ages 4–12 are invited to drop in for a free-build session using the library's LEGOs. No registration needed — walk-ins are always welcome.

9. 🎵 Sliver Live at Bridgeview Tavern Sat, November 14 · 8:00 PM · Bridgeview Tavern, Grand Island The band Sliver returns to Bridgeview Tavern for a live music night on the island.
