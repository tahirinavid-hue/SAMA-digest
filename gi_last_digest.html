Upcoming Events

  1. Grand Island Memorial Library Book Club · Tuesday

1. 📚 Grand Island Memorial Library Book Club Tuesday, September 8 · 6:00 PM · Grand Island Memorial Library, 1715 Bedell Rd

The evening book club meets on the second Tuesday of each month at 6:00 PM, and all are welcome. Physical copies of that month's title are available to borrow from the library.

3. 👨‍👩‍👧 Grand Island Memorial Library — "Sharing Kindness" Makerspace Program Tuesday, September 15 · 5:00–6:30 PM · Grand Island Memorial Library, 1715 Bedell Rd

The library is hosting a free "Sharing Kindness Makerspace Program" on Tuesday, September 15 from 5:00 to 6:30 PM, where families and individuals can bring a picture of their family, friends, or pets.

This is a drop-in, hands-on creative session open to all ages as part of the library's Welcoming Week activities.

4. 🎉 GISH Class of 1966 — 60th Reunion Dinner Saturday, September 19 · 6:00–11:00 PM · Grand Island Hotel/Casino

Tickets are expected to run approximately $50–$60 per person.
