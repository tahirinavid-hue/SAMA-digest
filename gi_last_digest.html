2. 🎵 Tuesday Summer Concert: Rock Angel Tuesday, Aug 04 · 6:30 PM · Marion Klingel Town Common, 2255 Baseline Rd. Rock Angel brings a high-energy set of rock, blues, and more to the Town Common stage.

The weekly concert series is free for the whole family, brought to you by the Grand Island Recreation Department.

3. 👨‍👩‍👧 National Night Out Tuesday, Aug 04 · 5:30–8:00 PM · Grand Island Town Hall This community-wide gathering is a chance to meet the police officers, firefighters, EMS personnel, and other first responders who keep Grand Island safe.

Local business owners, nonprofits, and service organizations will also be on hand to connect with neighbors.

4. 🎵 Tuesday Summer Concert: Yank the Cover Tuesday, Aug 11 · 6:30 PM · Marion Klingel Town Common, 2255 Baseline Rd. Yank the Cover, a premier WNY cover band, wraps up the 2026 summer concert series with an eclectic mix of classic rock, R&B, top 40, and country.

The show is free and open to all ages.

5. 📚 Library Book Club — "The Heaven & Earth Grocery Store" Tuesday, Aug 11 · 6:00 PM · Grand Island Memorial Library The monthly evening book club meets on the second Tuesday of each month, and all are welcome. Physical copies of the book are available to borrow from the library.

This month's title is The Heaven & Earth Grocery Store by James McBride.

6. 👨‍👩‍👧 End of Summer Bash Friday, Aug 14 · 1:00–3:00 PM · Grand Island Memorial Library An all-ages party at the library to close out the summer reading season, including Foam Fest from 1–2 pm.

The Ice Cream Social and Bubble Bash, originally part of the Summer Reading Kickoff, has been folded into this event as well.
