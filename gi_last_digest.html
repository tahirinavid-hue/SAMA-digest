1. 🎨 Adult Craft Workshop: Origami Boxes Saturday, Oct 3 · 10:00am–Noon · Grand Island Memorial Library Led by Adele Upton, this adults-only workshop walks participants through folding a 6-inch decorative gift box from scrapbooking paper. All materials are provided, but registration is required — call 716-773-7124.

2. 👨‍👩‍👧 Prime Time Preschool Saturday, Oct 3 · 11:00am · Grand Island Memorial Library (also Oct 10 & Oct 17) A recurring Saturday program for young children held at the library, part of a six-week series running through mid-October.

3. 🏛️ Town Board Workshop & Meeting Monday, Oct 5 · Grand Island Town Hall The Town Board holds its regular workshop at 6:30pm followed by the full board meeting at 8:00pm. Both are open to the public and also streamed live online.

4. 👨‍👩‍👧 Pajamatime Monday, Oct 5 · 6:15pm · Grand Island Memorial Library (also Oct 12) A cozy evening story program for little ones at the library — kids are welcome to show up in their pajamas and settle in for storytime before bed.

5. 🌿 WildWood WNY: Autumn Tree ID Tuesday, Oct 6 · 6:00pm–7:00pm · Grand Island Memorial Library A free program for budding nature detectives — participants learn how to identify local trees using clues like bark and leaf shape, led by WildWood WNY.

6. 👨‍👩‍👧 BookBop Tuesday, Oct 6 · 10:30am · Grand Island Memorial Library (also Oct 13) A fun children's program at the library that blends books with movement and playful engagement, great for young readers and wiggly listeners alike.

7. 👨‍👩‍👧 Preschool Storytime Wednesday, Oct 7 · 10:30am · Grand Island Memorial Library (also Oct 14) The library's weekly morning storytime for preschool-aged kids, featuring picture books and activities suited to the little crowd.

8. 🎨 Adult Crafternoon: Spooky Thrifted Paintings Saturday, Oct 10 · 1:00pm · Grand Island Memorial Library A Halloween-themed adult craft session where participants get creative with thrifted paintings and some spooky seasonal flair.

9. 🍽️ St. Timothy Drive-Thru Chiavetta's BBQ & Bake Sale Monday, Oct 12 · 3:30pm–6:30pm · St. Timothy Church, Grand Island Pick up Chiavetta's chicken from the comfort of your car, plus grab something sweet from the bake sale table — a popular annual fundraiser at St. Timothy's.

10. 📚 Evening Book Club: One Hundred Years of Solitude Tuesday, Oct 13 · 6:00pm · Grand Island Memorial Library The library's monthly evening book club meets to discuss Gabriel García Márquez's sweeping classic. Physical copies are available to borrow from the library ahead of the meeting.

11. 👨‍👩‍👧 Library Trunk or Treat Saturday, Oct 17 · Noon–2:00pm · Grand Island Memorial Library No registration needed to trick or treat among decorated car trunks, with crafts and family fun throughout. Those who want to set up a trunk can call 716-773-7124 and ask for the director by October 14th.
