1. 🎵 Tuesday Summer Concert: Yank the Cover Tuesday, August 11 · 6:30 PM · Marion Klingel Town Common, 2255 Baseline Rd

Yank the Cover is a WNY cover band that mixes classic rock, R&B, top 40, and country.

The concert is free and put on by the Grand Island Recreation Department.

The Zonta Club snack stand and a 50/50 raffle will be on site.

2. 📚 Library Book Club (Evening) Tuesday, August 11 · 6:00 PM · Grand Island Memorial Library

The evening book club meets monthly on the second Tuesday at 6:00 PM and is open to everyone.

The August pick is The Heaven & Earth Grocery Store by James McBride.

Physical copies are available to borrow from the branch ahead of the meeting.

3. 👨‍👩‍👧 End of Summer Bash: Foam Fest & Ice Cream Social Friday, August 14 · Grand Island Memorial Library

The Foam Fest summer kickoff event was rescheduled to Friday, August 14 as part of the library's End of Summer Bash.

The Ice Cream Social and Bubble Bash also moved to the same date.

Anyone who had a prior registration for the original July date should call the branch to confirm their spot.

4. 📚 Intro to A.I. Thursday, August 20 · 6:00–7:30 PM · Grand Island Memorial Library

This program covers how artificial intelligence shapes everyday life, from voice assistants to personalized recommendations, and introduces key AI concepts in simple, practical terms.

Registration is required — call 716-773-7124 to sign up.
