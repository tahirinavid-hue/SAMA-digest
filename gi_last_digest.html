1. 👨‍👩‍👧 Trunk or Treat at St. Timothy Lutheran Child Care Center Saturday, Oct 24 · 12:00 PM · 1453 Staley Road, Grand Island

A kid-friendly Halloween event at St. Timothy Lutheran, great for little ones looking to show off their costumes and collect some treats in a safe setting.

2. 🎉 Trinity Church Fall Festival Saturday, Oct 24 · Trinity Church, Grand Island

The festival features food, a Trunk or Treat, basket raffle, vendor crafts, and more.

A solid family outing with something for just about everyone.

3. 🍽️ Joe Synakowski Inaugural Chowder Sale Saturday, Oct 24 · Grand Island

The first-ever chowder sale honoring Joe Synakowski — a chance to grab a warm bowl and support a local community tradition in the making.

4. 🙌 GI Rotary Club Fall Clean-Up Saturday, Oct 24 · Grand Island

The Grand Island Rotary Club is out doing what it does — sprucing up the community before the cold rolls in. A good opportunity to pitch in alongside neighbors.

5. 🏃 Flow & Glow Yoga Saturday, Oct 24 · 2:30 PM · 2154 Grand Island Blvd, Grand Island

A glowing yoga session with over two dozen people already interested — bring your mat and wind down the weekend with some movement and good energy.

6. 🎨 Hand Lotion Workshop Sunday, Oct 25 · 2:00 PM · 2154 Grand Island Blvd, Grand Island

Make your own hand lotion at this hands-on DIY workshop — a fun and practical craft session heading into the dry-skin season.

7. 📚 GIHS PTSA Meeting Monday, Nov 2 · 5:30 PM · Grand Island High School Library, 1100 Ransom Road

The Grand Island High School PTSA holds its fall meeting — a good way for parents to stay connected with what's happening at the school and weigh in on upcoming plans.

8. 🏛️ GI Snowmobile Club General Meeting Wednesday, Nov 4 · 7:30 PM · Grand Island Rod & Gun Club, 1083 Whitehaven Road

The Snowmobile Club gathers for its monthly meeting as the season inches closer.

A NYS Snowmobile Safety Course is also coming up November 12 and 13 at the same location

— worth asking about if you have a young rider at home.

9. 📚 Friends of the Library Donated Book Sale Thursday, Nov 5 · 10:00 AM–8:00 PM (Members); Friday, Nov 6 · 10:00 AM–5:00 PM; Saturday, Nov 7 · 10:00 AM–3:00 PM (Bag Sale) · Grand Island Memorial Library, 1715 Bedell Road

All three days feature a bake sale running while supplies last, with all proceeds going to support the Grand Island Memorial Library.

Saturday's bag sale is the best deal — fill a bag for $5.
