1. 📚 Creating a Legacy Document Saturday, October 10 · 1:00 PM · 1715 Bedell Rd, Grand Island

This program helps participants think through and document important personal and family information for future generations.

A useful afternoon workshop for adults thinking about estate planning and personal records.

2. 🎉 BUR Bag Bingo Saturday, October 10 · 6:00 PM · Sandy Beach Yacht Club, Grand Island

BUR Bag Bingo is happening on Saturday, October 10 from 6:00 PM onward at Sandy Beach Yacht Club.

A fun community bingo night with a waterfront setting to wrap up your weekend.

3. 🌿 National Coming Out Day Walk Sunday, October 11 · 2:30 PM · Beaver Island State Park, Grand Island

Celebrate National Coming Out Day with an educator on this nature walk around Beaver Island State Park.

The walk is free and open to all.

4. 🍽️ St. Timothy Drive-Thru Chiavetta's BBQ & Bake Sale Monday, October 12 · 3:30–6:30 PM · 1453 Staley Rd, Grand Island

St. Timothy's Drive-Thru Chiavetta's BBQ and Bake Sale runs Monday, October 12th from 3:30 p.m. to 6:30 p.m.

A drive-thru format makes this a convenient weeknight pickup for the classic WNY chicken BBQ.

5. 👨‍👩‍👧 Kaegebein PTA Meeting Tuesday, October 13 · 3:30–4:30 PM · Kaegebein Elementary School, Grand Island

The Kaegebein PTA Meeting is scheduled for October 13 from 3:30 PM to 4:30 PM.

A good opportunity for Kaegebein families to connect with school leadership and stay current on upcoming school news.

6. 📚 Grand Island Memorial Library Book Club Tuesday, October 13 · 6:00 PM · Grand Island Memorial Library

The Evening Book Club meets monthly on 2nd Tuesdays at 6:00 PM; the October 13th selection is One Hundred Years of Solitude by Gabriel Garcia Marquez.

All are welcome and physical copies of the book are available to borrow from the library.

7. 🍽️ GICC Give Back Night Wednesday, October 14 · 5:00 PM · Dick and Jenny's, 1270 Baseline Rd, Grand Island

GICC Give Back Night takes place at Dick and Jenny's on Wednesday, October 14 starting at 5:00 PM.

Dining at this Grand Island favorite that evening helps support the Grand Island Chamber of Commerce.

8. 👨‍👩‍👧 Library Trunk or Treat Saturday, October 17 · Noon–2:00 PM · Grand Island Memorial Library

Trunk or Treat at the Grand Island Memorial Library runs Saturday, October 17th from noon to 2:00 PM, with no registration necessary, featuring trick or treating, crafts, and family fun.

Those interested in being a "trunker" can call 716-773-7124 and ask for the director by October 14th.

9. 🏛️ GI Historical Society Open House Sunday, October 18 · 2:00–4:00 PM · River Lea, Grand Island

The GI Historical Society Open House is Sunday, October 18 from 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., with items on display focused on Navy Island and Buckhorn Island.

It's a free afternoon look at some lesser-known chapters of Grand Island's past.

10. 📚 10 Warning Signs of Alzheimer's Wednesday, October 21 · Grand Island Golden Age Center

A program covering the 10 Warning Signs of Alzheimer's is scheduled for Wednesday, October 21, 2026 at the Golden Age Center.

The session is geared toward residents and caregivers looking to learn more about early recognition of the disease.

11. 🎉 Trinity Church Fall Festival Saturday, October 24 · Trinity Church, Grand Island

Trinity Church Fall Festival is Saturday, October 24th, with food, Trunk or Treat, basket raffle, vendor crafts and more.

A full community day with something for all ages, from shopping local crafts to trick-or-treating.

12. 👨‍👩‍👧 Trunk or Treat at St. Timothy Lutheran Child Care Center Saturday, October 24 · 12:00 PM · 1453 Staley Road, Grand Island

Trunk or Treat at St. Timothy Lutheran Child Care Center is on Saturday, October 24 at 12:00 PM at 1453 Staley Road, Grand Island.

A daytime Halloween event perfect for younger kids before the evening rush.
