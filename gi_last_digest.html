1. 👨‍👩‍👧 Halloween in the Library Sat, Oct 17 · Noon–2:00 PM · Grand Island Memorial Library A free drop-in Halloween party at the library featuring a mad scientist demonstration with bubbling, fizzing experiments and Halloween craft stations. No registration needed while supplies last.

2. 🏛️ GI Historical Society Open House Sun, Oct 18 · 2:00–4:00 PM · GI Historical Society The Historical Society opens its doors for an afternoon showing, with displays focused on the history of Navy Island and Buckhorn Island.

3. 🏛️ Town Board Workshop & Meeting Mon, Oct 19 · Town Hall, 2255 Baseline Rd. The Town Board holds both a 2027 budget workshop and its regular monthly meeting. Residents are welcome to attend and observe the public session.

4. 🎵 HS Choir and Orchestra Concert Mon, Oct 19 · 8:00–9:30 PM · GIHS Auditorium, 1100 Ransom Rd. Grand Island Senior High School's choir and orchestra take the stage for a fall evening concert. A great chance to hear student musicians perform before a live audience.

5. 📚 10 Warning Signs of Alzheimer's Wed, Oct 21 · Golden Age Center A community education program presented at the Golden Age Center covering early warning signs of Alzheimer's disease. Open to the public.

6. 🎨 Halloween Cooking Demo Sat, Oct 24 · 1:00–2:30 PM · Grand Island Memorial Library Presenter Liz Bauld leads a Halloween-themed cooking demonstration for adults (18+), complete with recipes and samples to try. Registration is recommended.

7. 👨‍👩‍👧 Trunk or Treat at St. Timothy Lutheran Child Care Center Sat, Oct 24 · 12:00 PM · 1453 Staley Road, Grand Island A family-friendly Halloween event hosted by St. Timothy Lutheran Child Care Center. Kids can collect candy from decorated car trunks in a safe, community setting.

8. 🎉 Trinity Church Fall Festival Sat, Oct 24 · Trinity Church, Grand Island Trinity Church's annual fall festival brings together food, a Trunk or Treat, a basket raffle, and vendor crafts. A solid outing for families looking for a full afternoon of fall fun.

9. 🏃 Flow & Glow Yoga Sat, Oct 24 · 2:30 PM · 2154 Grand Island Blvd. A yoga session with a glow-in-the-dark twist, held at the Grand Island Boulevard studio. Check with the organizer for registration details and any materials to bring.

10. 🎨 Hand Lotion Workshop Sun, Oct 25 · 2:00 PM · 2154 Grand Island Blvd. A hands-on workshop where participants make their own hand lotion to take home. Held at the same Grand Island Boulevard location as the yoga events — check for registration requirements.
